Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026 · Applies to hypa-labs.com and Thoughts

The short version. Our apps don't have accounts. What you tell Thoughts about yourself stays on your phone. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. This website doesn't use cookies or analytics.

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Hypa Labs Ltd ("Hypa Labs", "we", "us") handles personal data when you use our website at hypa-labs.com, our apps (currently Thoughts for iPhone), or contact us. Together we call these our "services".

We are the data controller for the personal data described here. We're a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17093236). Our full details are on our legal notice page.

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the relevant app.

2. Children

Our services are not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used one of our apps and we hold data about them, email us at hello@hypa-labs.com and we'll delete it.

3. The personal data we collect

When you visit our website

Our website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and it loads no third-party fonts or scripts. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically processes your IP address and basic browser information in its server logs to deliver the page and keep the service secure.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your name and email address, the content of your message, and anything you choose to send us, such as a portfolio or links to your accounts.

When you use Thoughts

Stored only on your phone. During setup, you can answer a few questions so the app can choose writing that fits you: the name you'd like to be called, your age range, how you've been feeling lately, why you're using the app, the topics you choose, and when you'd like to receive thoughts. Your saved thoughts and your streak are stored on your phone too. We don't receive any of this, it isn't linked to your identity, and it's deleted if you delete the app.

Processed to run the app:

  • Purchase information. Apple handles all payments. We never see your card details, name, email address or Apple ID. Apple tells us, through our subscription provider, whether you have an active subscription or purchase. This involves an anonymous app-generated identifier, transaction details (product, price, dates, renewal status), your IP address and device information such as model, iOS version, app version, language, region and App Store country.
  • Subscription screen. Which options were shown and selected, together with your IP address and the same device information, linked to the anonymous identifier, so we can see whether the screen works.
  • Usage events. Anonymous events such as whether setup was completed, when a free trial starts or converts, and streak milestones. These don't include your setup answers.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. Device model, iOS version, app version and what the app was doing when a problem happened.
  • Network data. Your IP address, processed by our providers whenever the app connects to them, for example to download new content or check your subscription. We don't use it to identify you or work out your precise location.

What we never collect

Our apps don't ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone.

4. How we collect it

  • From you, when you email us or answer questions in an app (those answers stay on your phone).
  • Automatically, through the app and our service providers when you use the app or visit the website.
  • From Apple, which confirms purchases and subscription status, and may share crash reports if you've turned on sharing with app developers in your iPhone settings.

8. How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone: until you delete the app.
  • Purchase records: while you have a subscription or purchase, then as long as needed for tax and accounting (usually six years under UK law).
  • Usage and crash data: up to 24 months.
  • Emails: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, unless we go on to work together.
  • Website server logs: as set by our hosting provider, usually a short period.

After that, we delete the data or make it anonymous.

9. How we protect it

We keep the data we handle to a minimum. Information sent between our apps and our providers is encrypted in transit, and access to our systems is limited to the people who need it. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and will act quickly if something goes wrong.

10. Your rights and choices

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to data portability.

  • Notifications: turn them off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Data on your phone: delete the app to remove it.
  • Sharing crash reports with developers: controlled in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

To exercise your rights, email hello@hypa-labs.com. Because our apps don't have accounts, most of what we hold can't be linked to you without your help, so we may ask for details such as the order ID from your App Store receipt. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. If you're in the EU, you can contact your local data protection authority.
