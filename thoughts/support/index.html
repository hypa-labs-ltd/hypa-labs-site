How do I cancel my subscription?
  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Thoughts, then Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or current period ends to avoid being charged again. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

All payments go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple rather than by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, signing in with the Apple ID you used to buy.

I've reinstalled the app or got a new phone. How do I get my subscription back?

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with, open Thoughts and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in Settings. Your access comes back straight away. You won't be charged again.

Thoughts doesn't have accounts yet, so saved thoughts and your streak are stored on your phone and don't move to a new device.

How does the free trial work?

The free trial is available on the yearly plan. You get full access, including widgets, for the trial period. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the yearly subscription starts and you're charged. Each Apple ID can use the trial once.

How do I add the widget?
  1. Long press an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget (or the + button).
  3. Search for Thoughts, choose a size, and tap Add Widget.

For the Lock Screen, long press your Lock Screen, tap Customise, choose Lock Screen, then tap the widget area and select Thoughts.

My widget isn't showing a new thought.

Open the Thoughts app once. This refreshes the thoughts your widget has queued up. If it still doesn't update, remove the widget and add it again.

If your subscription or trial has ended, the widget shows only the Thoughts logo until you resubscribe.

I'm not getting notifications.

Go to Settings, then Notifications, then Thoughts, and check Allow Notifications is on. Also check Focus modes aren't silencing them. You can change how often and when you receive thoughts in the app's settings.

How do I change the name or answers I gave during setup?

Your setup answers are stored only on your phone. You can update your preferences in the app's settings.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone, including your setup answers, saved thoughts and streak. For any other data relating to you, such as purchase records held by our subscription provider, email us and we'll handle it. See our privacy policy for more detail.

I'm going through something difficult.

Thoughts isn't a replacement for real support. If you're struggling or feel unsafe, please talk to someone. In the UK, Samaritans are free to call on 116 123, any time. Outside the UK, findahelpline.com lists free services in your country. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
